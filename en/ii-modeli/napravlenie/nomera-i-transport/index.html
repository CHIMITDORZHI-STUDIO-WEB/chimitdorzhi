Open models for license plates and vehicles

These models read a license plate from a camera, tell vehicle types apart and count traffic. They go on barriers and entrances, in warehouses, car parks, car washes and logistics. One caveat: filming people and vehicles and storing such footage is personal data handling; the rules and retention periods are a question for a lawyer.
