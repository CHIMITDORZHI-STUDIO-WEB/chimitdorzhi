Open-source models for robotics

Robotics models connect vision, instruction understanding and action: they teach an arm to grasp objects and a mobile platform to navigate. This is a field for warehouses, manufacturing and research projects. Check which hardware the model supports, whether fine-tuning is needed and what the license allows.
