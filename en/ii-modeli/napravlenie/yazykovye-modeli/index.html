Open-source language models for business

Language models write and edit text, answer customers, summarize documents and power chatbots and assistants. An open model can run on your own infrastructure, so data never leaves your company. When choosing, check support for the languages you need, whether the license allows commercial use, and which model size your hardware can handle.
