Open-source medical AI models

Medical models help analyze images, clinical notes and discharge summaries and answer questions over clinical materials. They support doctors and clinic staff and do not replace a diagnosis. Always check the license, terms for medical use, data protection requirements and whether the model can be deployed on-premises.
