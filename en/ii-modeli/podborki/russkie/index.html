Open-source AI models with Russian language support

This collection lists open models whose developers declare Russian language support for text, speech, documents and search. It is a starting point for projects serving Russian-speaking customers or handling Russian documents. Declared support does not guarantee quality, so test the model on your own examples and check the license and hardware requirements.
