Open-source alternative to Notion AI: document work inside your company

Notion AI handles the routine around documents: summarizing a long memo, answering from the knowledge base, pulling clauses out of a contract. Open models cover the same routine on your own server: internal documents never go to someone else's cloud, there is no volume cap, and the model can be fine-tuned on your templates or made to fill a JSON schema you define — client cards, fields from statements. Some of these models are built for document work rather than chat, and they do not demand heavy hardware. What you do not get is the editor, collaboration and access control — that is the product you build around the model. And a summary is still a retelling, so figures and deadlines from a contract are verified against the original.

Sber open models with strong Russian language support and local context, from 10B-A1.8B to 702B, all MIT. GigaChat3.1-Audio handles recordings up to two hours; GFusion is a fast diffusion text version.

  • Russian-language employee assistant on your own server

  • Working with contracts and internal policies

NuExtract

NuMind · France

Models for template-based data extraction: give it a document or scan and a JSON field template, get a filled-in JSON back. NuExtract3 (4B) also converts scans to Markdown.

  • Extracting company details, amounts and dates from invoices and contracts into JSON

Google · USA

Compact Google models that run well on a single computer; larger versions understand images. Includes CodeGemma for code, FunctionGemma 270M for function calling and the fast DiffusionGemma.
