Open-source alternative to Photoshop and Canva AI: photo editing and backgrounds

Bulk photo work — product cards, site photos, shoot archives — is manual labour in an editor and, in cloud services, runs into per-image billing plus uploading client material to a third party. Open models cover the individual operations: strip the background, upscale and clean up an old photo, remove an unwanted object or caption, restore a face on a blurry shot. All of it installs on your own server and can be wired into a pipeline that processes a thousand cards without a designer, while the originals stay with you. What does not appear is a general-purpose editor: each model does one thing and chaining them into a process is your work. On hard edges — hair, fur, transparent and reflective surfaces — results are uneven, and part of the output still goes to a designer for cleanup.

What to use instead

Photo editingGGUF2024–2025

BiRefNet

Nankai University · China

An open MIT-licensed model for precise object segmentation and background removal. RMBG-2.0 is built on it. Versions for 2K and for hair and semi-transparent edges.

  • Bulk background removal from product photos
  • Precise masks for design and print
  • Cutting out people with hair for advertising
