Open-source alternative to Google Translate and DeepL: translation on your own server

Contracts, supplier correspondence and texts the company never meant to publish all pass through online translators. Open translation models remove that exposure: they run on your own server, nothing leaves the perimeter, volume is unlimited, and the model can be fine-tuned on your own terminology — product names, legal wording — which a cloud translator will not do. Small single-pair models run on a CPU, so heavy hardware is not always required. The downsides are real: on colloquial speech, idioms and dense legal phrasing open translation trails mature commercial services, and an important document still needs a human read before it goes out. Rare language pairs are covered unevenly, so quality has to be tested on your own texts.
