Open-source alternative to ElevenLabs: run text-to-speech yourself

ElevenLabs is bought for voicing videos, auto-replies and voice messages. Open speech models install on your own server: scripts and voice recordings are never handed over, you can synthesize without limits, and some models run on an ordinary CPU with no GPU at all. Several of them clone a voice from a short sample, so you can keep one consistent brand voice instead of re-recording every time a line changes. The weak spots are familiar: intonation flattens out on long or emotional copy, stress in names and technical terms often needs manual correction, and live dialogue requires real tuning. And one issue is legal rather than technical: another person's voice must not be cloned without their written consent.
